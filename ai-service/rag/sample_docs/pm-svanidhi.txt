PM SVANidhi is a working-capital credit scheme for urban street vendors (launched 1 June
2020). Under PM SVANidhi 2.0 (Cabinet approval, 27 August 2025) the scheme runs to 31 March
2030 with a total outlay of Rs 7,332 crore and about 1.15 crore beneficiaries, roughly 50
lakh of them new. Many migrant workers who become street vendors in cities benefit from
this scheme.

Eligibility:
- Street vendors, hawkers, and footpath vendors who are selling goods from fixed or mobile 
  structures in urban areas.
- Vendors who possess a Certificate of Registration/Identity Card issued by the Urban Local 
  Body (ULB) or Town Vending Committee (TVC).
- Existing beneficiaries plus new vendors under the 2.0 expansion (about 50 lakh new
  beneficiaries planned per the August 2025 Cabinet decision).

Loan details:
- First loan: up to Rs 15,000 (raised from Rs 10,000 under PM SVANidhi 2.0), repayable
  within about a year.
- Second loan: up to Rs 25,000 (was Rs 20,000), available after repaying the first loan
  on time.
- Third loan: up to Rs 50,000, available after repaying the second loan on time.
- Subsidy: 7% per annum interest subsidy is credited directly to the vendor's bank account 
  after repayment of the loan.

Digital transactions incentive:
- Digital payments: an incentive is given for digital transactions during the loan period;
  incentive terms have changed over time — confirm the current rates on the official portal.

How to apply:
- Visit the PM SVANidhi portal: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
- Apply online with Aadhaar card, bank account details, and ULB/TVC registration certificate.
- Alternatively, apply through Common Service Centres (CSCs) or bank branches.
- The loan is processed through banks, NBFCs, and MFIs partnered with the scheme.

Registration for street vendors:
- To get a ULB/TVC certificate, approach the local Urban Local Body or Town Vending Committee.
- Provide proof of vending (self-declaration or photographs), Aadhaar card, and address proof.
- The certificate is issued free of cost.
