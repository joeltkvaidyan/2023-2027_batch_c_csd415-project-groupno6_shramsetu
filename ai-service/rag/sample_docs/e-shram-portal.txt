The e-Shram portal is the National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW), created so that migrant
workers, construction workers, gig workers, domestic workers, street vendors, agricultural labourers
and other unorganised sector workers can register and receive a UAN (Universal Account Number) card.

Who can register: Any home-based, self-employed or wage worker in the unorganised
sector aged 16 years or above who is not a member of EPFO or ESIC. There is no
income criterion and registration is free.

Benefits of the e-Shram card:
- A 12-digit Universal Account Number valid across India, portable if the worker migrates to another state.
- Since 21 October 2024 the portal hosts a One-Stop-Solution (OSS) scheme tracker
  for central welfare schemes; it does not automatically enrol anyone or guarantee
  a benefit.
- Accident insurance is not automatic: enrol separately in the PM Suraksha Bima
  Yojana through a savings bank account (Rs 2 lakh for accidental death/total
  disability, Rs 1 lakh for partial disability, Rs 20 per year).
- Access to a self-declared employment history that can be shown to employers or government offices.

How to register: Workers can self-register using the eshram.gov.in website or mobile app using their
Aadhaar-linked mobile number, or visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with their Aadhaar card
and bank account details free of cost.

Documents required: Aadhaar number, mobile number linked to Aadhaar (or use of Aadhaar OTP/biometric
authentication at a CSC if the number is not linked), and bank account details for benefit transfer.

Grievance redressal: Workers can call the e-Shram helpline number 14434 (toll-free) for help in
registration or to report issues with their card.
