Anganwadi workers and helpers are frontline workers in the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) scheme. They play a crucial role in providing health, nutrition, and early childhood education services. This document covers their rights, entitlements, and welfare schemes.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Monthly Honorarium: Anganwadi workers receive monthly honorarium (not wages) as per State Government norms.
2. Maternity Leave: Anganwadi workers are entitled to maternity leave as per government rules.
3. Training: Regular training programmes for skill upgradation.
4. Social Security: Access to PM-SYM, PM-SBY, and other social security schemes.
5. Insurance: Group insurance coverage for Anganwadi workers.

ENTITLEMENTS:
1. Anganwadi Worker: monthly honorarium set by the STATE government (amounts differ by
   state and cadre; no verified single national figure — check your state WCD department)
2. Anganwadi Helper (Sahayika): monthly honorarium set by the state government
3. Mini Anganwadi Worker: Monthly honorarium
4. Additional incentives for performance

SOCIAL SECURITY:
1. PM-SYM: Rs 3,000/month pension after 60 (entry age 18-40, monthly income up to Rs 15,000)
2. PM-SBY: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance
3. Ayushman Bharat: Rs 5 lakh health insurance
4. e-Shram Registration: For additional social security benefits

DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. Supplementary nutrition for children (6 months to 6 years) and pregnant/lactating mothers
2. Immunization of children and pregnant women
3. Health check-up and referral services
4. Pre-school non-formal education
5. Nutrition and health education for women
6. Home visits for new-born babies and pregnant women
7. Maintenance of records and registers

MIGRANT ANGANWADI WORKERS:
- Anganwadi workers are typically recruited locally
- Migrant women can benefit from ICDS services at their place of residence
- Children of migrant workers are entitled to Anganwadi services
- Nutrition supplements are available at Anganwadi centres
