The One Nation One Ration Card scheme ensures that migrant workers and their families can access 
foodgrains from any Fair Price Shop (FPS) across India, regardless of the state they are currently 
living in. This is critical for migrant workers who move between states for work.

Eligibility: Any household that is a beneficiary under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 
including Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) and Priority Households (PHH), is eligible. The ration 
card must be linked with Aadhaar of all family members.

How it works:
- The ration card is linked to Aadhaar (seeding is mandatory).
- When a worker migrates to another state, they can visit any FPS in that state and draw their 
  entitled foodgrains using Aadhaar authentication (fingerprint or iris scan).
- The entitlement follows the worker: 5 kg of foodgrain per person per month under NFSA, and 
  35 kg per AAY family per month.
- Under PMGKAY the regular NFSA quota (5 kg/person/month; 35 kg for AAY families) is FREE
  for five years from 1 January 2024; outside PMGKAY the statutory NFSA issue prices apply
  (rice Rs 3/kg, wheat Rs 2/kg, coarse grains Rs 1/kg).

How to link Aadhaar with ration card:
- Visit the local FPS or the state's Food and Civil Supplies department website.
- Many states allow online seeding through the state PDS portal or via Aadhaar Seeding Kiosk 
  at Common Service Centres (CSCs).
- Check Aadhaar-linking status through your state PDS portal or a Common Service Centre.

Helpline: 1967 (PDS grievances) or 14457 (Anna Sahayata); e-Shram queries: 14434.

Important for migrants: If your ration card is from your home state but you are working in 
another state, you can still draw rations in the work state. However, the quantity drawn in the 
work state will be adjusted against your home state entitlement.
