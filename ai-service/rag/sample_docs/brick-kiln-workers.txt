TITLE: Rights and Welfare of Brick Kiln Workers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2025-02-15

Brick kiln workers are among the most vulnerable sections of the unorganised workforce in India. They work in extreme conditions and often face exploitation. This document covers the rights, legal protections, and welfare schemes available to brick kiln workers.

KEY RIGHTS:
1. Minimum Wages: Brick kiln workers are entitled to minimum wages as fixed by respective State Governments.
2. Payment of Wages: Wages must be paid on time without arbitrary deductions (timely wage payment is now governed by the Code on Wages, 2019, effective 21 November 2025).
3. Safe Working Conditions: Employers must provide safe working environment including proper ventilation, drinking water, and rest areas.
4. PPE: Employers must provide personal protective equipment including masks, gloves, and safety shoes.
5. Medical Facilities: First aid and medical facilities must be available at the workplace.
6. Housing: no verified current-law entitlement — confirm any living-arrangement support
   through the employer or the local labour office.
7. Children's Education: Children of brick kiln workers must be provided free education under RTE Act.
8. Maternity Benefit: Women workers are entitled to maternity benefits under the Code on Social Security, 2020 (which replaced the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961).

SOCIAL SECURITY:
1. e-Shram Registration: Brick kiln workers can register on e-Shram portal
2. PM-SYM: Rs 3,000/month pension after 60 (entry age 18-40, monthly income up to Rs 15,000)
3. PM-SBY: Rs 2 lakh accident insurance (separate enrolment via a savings bank account)
4. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY): Rs 5 lakh hospital cover for eligible families
5. State labour departments run separate schemes in some states — the construction
   workers' boards do not administer brick-kiln work by default.

MIGRANT BRICK KILN WORKERS:
- Migrant workers form the majority of brick kiln workforce
- They are protected under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, which replaced the Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979
Protections in the Code-era framework: portal registration and safety obligations apply;
the old 1979 Act's journey/accommodation/displacement claims have NO verified successor —
confirm current provisions with the labour department. Heat guidance: the 28 April 2026
nationwide heatwave advisory asked States/UTs to give special attention to brick-kiln,
construction and daily-wage workers (advisory only, not a benefit).

REGULATION OF BRICK KILNS:
- Brick kilns must be registered with the local authority
- Inspections by Inspector-cum-Facilitator
- Employment of children below 14 years is strictly prohibited
- Bonded labour is prohibited under the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act

WELFARE SCHEMES:
1. State labour departments: some states run welfare schemes for kiln workers — ask locally
2. PM-SVANidhi: For workers who are also street vendors
3. PM Garib Kalyan Yojana: free foodgrains for NFSA beneficiaries under PMGKAY (since
   1 January 2024)
4. Skill India/PMKVY: Free skill training for career advancement
