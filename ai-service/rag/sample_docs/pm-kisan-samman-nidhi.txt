PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) — Income Support for Farmers

PM-KISAN provides direct income support of Rs 6,000 per year to all landholding farmer
families (the small-and-marginal-only cap was removed in 2019), paid in three equal
instalments of Rs 2,000 each.

Key Features:
- Rs 6,000 per year transferred directly to farmer's bank account
- Three instalments: Rs 2,000 each in April-July, August-November, December-March
- Money transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Enrolment totals are updated on the official portal (roughly 11 crore families as of
  late 2025).

Eligibility:
- All farmer families with cultivable land holding
- The 2019 change opened the scheme to ALL landholding farmer families (the original
  small-and-marginal-only limit no longer applies).
- Subject to certain exclusions

Excluded Categories:
- Institutional landholders
- Former and current constitutional post holders
- All serving and retired government employees.
- Income taxpayers and professionals paying income tax
- Professionals registered with professional bodies

Registration Process:
1. Visit nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) with land records
2. Or register online at https://pmkisan.gov.in
3. Provide Aadhaar number, bank account details, land records
4. After verification, instalments are credited automatically

Documents Required:
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details (account number and IFSC code)
- Land records / Khatauni
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

Checking Status:
1. Visit https://pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click on "Beneficiary Status"
3. Enter Aadhaar number or bank account number
4. View instalment status
