TITLE: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) — Income Support for Farmers
DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
LAST_UPDATED: 2026-11-05
SOURCE_URL: https://pmkisan.gov.in
LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-11-03
AUTHORITY: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
STATUS: current
---
PM-KISAN provides direct income support of Rs 6,000 per year to all landholding farmer
families (the small-and-marginal-only cap was removed in 2019), paid in three equal
instalments of Rs 2,000 each. This scheme is relevant for migrant workers who are also
farmers or who come from agricultural families.

Key Features:
- Rs 6,000 per year transferred directly to farmer's bank account
- Three instalments: Rs 2,000 each in April-July, August-November, December-March
- Money transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- The benefit is per farmer FAMILY, not per individual: spouses in the same household are
  one family (state land-record rules decide what counts as a separate family).
- Enrolment totals are updated on the official portal (roughly 11 crore families as of
  late 2025).

Eligibility:
- All farmer families with cultivable land holding
- The 2019 change opened the scheme to ALL landholding farmer families (the original
  small-and-marginal-only limit no longer applies).
- Subject to certain exclusions

Excluded Categories:
- Institutional landholders (companies, trusts, societies)
- Former and current constitutional post holders
- All serving and retired government employees.
- Income taxpayers and professionals paying income tax
- Professionals registered with professional bodies (doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered
  accountants)
- Landless households are NOT eligible — the benefit follows land ownership, not residence
  or work.

For migrant workers:
- A migrant who owns cultivable land in the home state can register from anywhere; the
  benefit follows the land, not the work state, and is not location-dependent.
- Aadhaar and the bank account must be linked for the instalment to be credited.
- A landless migrant worker should be pointed to the schemes that do not require land
  ownership instead of PM-KISAN.

Registration Process:
1. Visit nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) with land records
2. Or register online at https://pmkisan.gov.in ("New Farmer Registration")
3. Provide Aadhaar number, bank account details, land records
4. After verification, instalments are credited automatically
5. Land records can be updated through the "Updation of Land Records" section on the portal

Documents Required:
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details (account number and IFSC code)
- Land records / Khatauni
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar

Checking Status:
1. Visit https://pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click on "Beneficiary Status"
3. Enter Aadhaar number or bank account number
4. View instalment status

- PM-KISAN office line: 011-23382401
