The National Workers' Education Scheme is a Ministry of Labour and Employment programme
run through the Central and State Workers' Education Boards to educate workers on their
rights, welfare schemes and skills — with a special focus on workers in the unorganised
sector, including migrant workers.

What it covers:
- Awareness training on labour rights and welfare schemes (e-Shram, EPF/ESIC, ration
  cards, and the minimum-wage and timely-payment rules of the Code on Wages, 2019).
- Health and safety at the workplace (now under the OSH Code, 2020).
- Financial literacy and digital payments.
- Skill-development and entrepreneurship referrals to schemes such as PMKVY 4.0 (ages
  15-45; free short-term training and Recognition of Prior Learning through Skill India
  Digital — enrolment is phased, so check the current window before promising a stipend
  or a training slot).

How to participate:
- Contact the Workers' Education Board office for your state (contacts on labour.gov.in).
- Programmes for workers are typically free; schedules vary by state and year.
- The District Labour Office or a Common Service Centre can point you to current
  programmes.

For migrant workers:
- Learn your rights and entitlements in the state where you work.
- Use the e-Shram One-Stop-Solution (on eshram.gov.in since 21 October 2024) to see which
  central welfare schemes you may qualify for.

Contact:
- Skill guidance: National Career Service portal (ncs.gov.in), helpline 1514.
- Grievances: CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) or samadhan.labour.gov.in.
- Ministry of Labour and Employment: labour.gov.in
