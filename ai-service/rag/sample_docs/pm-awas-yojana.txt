Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) — Housing for All

PMAY aims to provide pucca houses with basic amenities to eligible families. It has two
components: PMAY-Urban and PMAY-Gramin (phase targets and dates follow the official scheme
guidelines; check pmaymis.gov.in / pmayg.nic.in for the current phase).

Key Features:
- Financial assistance for constructing or buying a house
- Interest subsidy on home loans (Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme)
- Direct Benefit Transfer to beneficiary's bank account
- Houses with basic amenities: water supply, sewerage, electricity

PMAY-Gramin (Rural):
- Rs 1.20 lakh assistance for plain areas
- Rs 1.30 lakh assistance for hilly/difficult areas
- House size: minimum 25 sq metres built-up area
- Material assistance: cement, steel, bricks provided

PMAY-Urban:
- Interest-subsidy bands under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme have changed since 2024;
  verify the current sub-scheme and amounts at pmaymis.gov.in before advising a borrower.
- Construction support and beneficiary-led construction

Eligibility:
- Families without a pucca house, per the scheme's notified guidelines (income-category
  definitions have been revised; confirm current categories on pmaymis.gov.in/
  pmayg.nic.in).
- Selection runs through the state housing survey / permanent wait lists (Awas+ for
  PMAY-G), not through e-Shram registration.
- Migrant workers usually apply in their HOME state through the survey/wait-list process;
  an e-Shram card helps identification but is not a PMAY entry route by itself.
- Women heads of household, SC/ST, persons with disabilities and minorities get priority
  per the scheme guidelines.

How to Apply:
1. Visit https://pmaymis.gov.in (Urban) or https://pmayg.nic.in (Rural)
2. Or visit nearest CSC with required documents
3. Fill application form with family and income details
4. Submit Aadhaar, income certificate, bank details
5. Wait for verification against the survey/wait-list and approval
6. Once approved, construction can begin

Documents Required:
- Aadhaar card of all family members
- Income certificate from Tehsildar/SDM
- Bank account details
- BPL certificate or ration card
- Land ownership documents (if own land)
- Affidavit that family does not own a pucca house

Helpline:
- Portals: pmaymis.gov.in (urban) and pmayg.nic.in (rural); confirm the current helpline
  numbers on the official sites before quoting them.
