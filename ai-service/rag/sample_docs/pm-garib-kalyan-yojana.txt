The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) delivers FREE foodgrains through the
public distribution system (PDS) to NFSA beneficiaries: 5 kg per person per month (35 kg per
family for AAY households) for five years from 1 January 2024, covering about 81.35 crore
beneficiaries.

Key provisions for migrant workers:
- Free foodgrains: 5 kg per person per month to ALL NFSA beneficiaries (AAY families: 35 kg
  per family). The COVID-era extras (e.g. extra chana/daal) were time-bound and ended.
- Coverage: current PMGKAY covers existing NFSA beneficiary lists; new ration cards are
  issued through the state NFSA process, not automatically via ONORC.
- Duration: the current five-year PMGKAY phase began 1 January 2024; further extensions are
  announced by government decisions (check dfpd.gov.in).

How migrant workers benefit:
- Migrant workers WITHOUT a ration card cannot draw NFSA foodgrains from the work state:
  they must hold a ration card (apply at the local ration office; a 2023 Supreme Court order
  directs states to extend cards to eligible non-cardholders).
- Those with ration cards from other states can draw their entitled quantity at any FPS in the 
  state where they are currently working.
- The free-of-cost regular quota is available to all NFSA beneficiaries wherever they live
  (portability via ONORC when drawing in another state).

Registration requirements:
- Aadhaar is used to authenticate the ration drawal at the FPS ePoS device.
- Ration card (if available) from the home state.
- If no ration card: apply at the local ration office; ePoS registration happens only after
  the state issues a card.

Grievance redressal:
- Call the toll-free PDS helpline 1967.
- File complaints on the state Food and Civil Supplies portal.
- State PDS portals list FPS locations; use the 1967 (PDS) / 14457 (Anna Sahayata) helplines.
